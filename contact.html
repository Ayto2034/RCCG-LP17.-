Before you visit

Frequently asked questions

What should I wear to a service?+

Come as you are. Many people dress smart for Sunday service, but you'll always be welcome exactly as you are.

Is there somewhere for my children?+

Yes — Children's Church runs alongside the main service so the whole family can worship together.

Can I join if I can't attend in person?+

Yes — services are shared online. Check our Facebook page, @rccglp17hq, for the live link and updates each week.

How do I join a ministry or fellowship group?+

Speak with an usher or minister after any service, or message us on Facebook and we'll connect you to the right group.

What happens at the Monthly Provincial Vigil?+

A province-wide night of prayer held every 3rd Friday at 10:00 PM at the provincial headquarters — open to everyone.

How do I become a member?+

Visit us for a service and let the welcome team know — they'll walk you through the next steps from there.
